The problem (reported 2026-10-21)
---------------------------------
Test strip 700 mm long, 1 mm magnelis, bent centred in the machine:
  far right end:  92.5 degrees
  far left end:   102 degrees
CORRECTION: the angles are measured as bend deviation (inverted), so a LARGER number = MORE bent.
The left end (Y1, left cylinder) is the DEEP one, the right end is shallower.
Estimated depth difference about 0.5 mm (about 0.055 mm per degree for 1 mm steel, die 3008).
Presets Y1 and Y2 differ by only 0.06 mm, so the presets alone do not explain it.

Plan (from the discussion)
--------------------------
1. Rule out strip and tools: bend a strip, turn it end for end and bend again. Check tool seating.
2. Find the exact correction with a test program: make Y1 SHALLOWER (left end is too deep) in 0.1-0.2 mm steps,
   measure both ends.
3. Then move the same amount into the Y1 preset (DATA UPDATE), in steps of 0.05-0.1 mm. Test the direction
   first. For a shallower Y1 the ACTUAL Y1 reading should become LOWER at the same position (the ram stops higher).
4. Keep the total Y1 change under about 0.5 mm. If the parallelism light goes out or a fault appears,
   restore the original preset and stop.

Cause (reported 2026-10-21)
--------------------------
The left linear scale (Y1) was removed on 2026-10-20 to be cleaned and mounted back the same way.
The angle error appeared after that, so the Y1 origin (reference) shifted when the scale was remounted.
Fix: correct the Y1 preset, after checking the scale mounting (see plan). Diagnostic: compare ACTUAL Y1 and Y2 at the
top and at the bend point; a constant difference means a pure offset (preset), a difference that changes with the
height means the scale is mounted tilted or its head clearance is wrong.
